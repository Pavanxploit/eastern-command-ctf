# Investigation Workflow

## Before analysis
- Confirm scope and permission.
- Preserve originals; analyze copies.
- Record file names, sizes, timestamps, and hashes where useful.
- Work in an isolated environment for potentially malicious files.

## During analysis
- Maintain a timestamped notebook.
- Record commands, tool versions, and outputs.
- Separate direct observations from interpretations.
- Corroborate important claims with more than one source where possible.
- Avoid treating a single alert or artifact as proof without context.

## Reporting
- State the question and scope.
- Explain why each analytical step was performed.
- Tie each conclusion to evidence.
- Include uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations.
- Redact private information.
- Ensure another analyst can reproduce the reasoning.

## Publication review
- Review event rules and organizer permissions.
- Remove original archives, malware, credentials, private emails, private links, and personal data.
- Check screenshots and commit history for accidental disclosures.
- Do not publish restricted flags or full solutions if prohibited.
