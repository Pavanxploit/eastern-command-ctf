# Round 1 — Threat Intelligence & Security Analytics

## Assessment scope

The assessment brief described three tasks:

1. **Malware analysis** — examine the supplied artifacts and document verified observations.
2. **Log analysis** — determine which account/system was compromised, the likely attack path, and supporting evidence.
3. **Audio analysis** — denoise and transcribe the supplied intercepted audio.

## Write-ups

Create one report per task, or one combined report with clearly separated sections. Use `reports/report-template.md` as a starting point.

## Evidence handling

- Do not commit the original `victim_artifacts.7z`, `web_activity.7z`, or `war-intercept.wav` files unless you have explicit permission.
- Do not publish private submission emails, personal details, or challenge credentials.
- Redact usernames, email addresses, tokens, internal IPs, and other sensitive details where required.
- Include only screenshots and findings that the organizers permit you to share.

## Findings

Add your verified conclusions here only after checking them against your original notes and evidence. Do not invent IOCs, compromised identities, malware behavior, or transcript text.
